Two common situations call for a quick step from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: turn the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so the moving air thaws the coil, because running it frozen can overflow the drain pan and hurt the compressor. The second is a furnace that short-cycles or gives off a burning smell. Turn it off at the service switch and give us a call. Those few minutes can save you from a much larger repair.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Nashville

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Take a look at the age of your system. That number tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and is also showing rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

Small problems rarely stay small for long. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a miserable night.
