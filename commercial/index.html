Kitchen Climate Control

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort all interact, and a system that's undersized or failing shows up first as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines pour huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air straight out of the building. Our food-service work covers rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. If a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, drags hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and leaves staff miserable during the rush. Our technicians balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits your operating schedule. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and guests comfortable in the meantime.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Clarksville HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent service schedule.
